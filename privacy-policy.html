Privacy Policy - OpenInApp

Last updated: February 22, 2026

Introduction

OpenInApp ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our app handles information when you use it.

Information We Collect

OpenInApp does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All data stays on your device.

  • Link History: URLs you open through the app are stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device.
  • Pinned Links: Links you pin/favorite are stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device.
  • Clipboard Data: When you tap the Paste button, the app reads your clipboard content. This data is only used to fill the URL input field and is not stored or transmitted anywhere.

Internet Usage

The app uses internet access solely for the following purpose:

  • Deep Link Resolution: When you open a URL, the app may fetch the website's assetlinks.json file to determine which installed apps can handle the link. This is a standard Android mechanism. No personal data is sent in these requests.
  • Redirect Resolution: For shortened URLs (e.g., onelink.me, app.link), the app follows redirects to find the actual destination. No personal data is sent.

Installed Apps

The app uses the QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission to discover which apps on your device can open a given URL. This information is used only to show you the app chooser dialog and is never transmitted or stored beyond the current session.

Data Storage

All app data (link history, pinned links) is stored locally on your device using Android's SharedPreferences. You can clear this data at any time by:

  • Clearing the app's data from Android Settings
  • Uninstalling the app

Third-Party Services

OpenInApp does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app has zero external dependencies.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any user data with third parties. No data leaves your device.

Children's Privacy

OpenInApp does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13 years of age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
