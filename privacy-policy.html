Privacy Policy — OpenInApp

Last updated: April 21, 2026

OpenInApp respects your privacy. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information We Collect

OpenInApp does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. All app data stays on your device.

Data Stored Locally

The app stores the following on your device only:

  • Link history (URLs you opened through the app)
  • Pinned links

You can clear this data any time from Android Settings or by uninstalling the app.

Clipboard

When you tap the Paste button, the app reads the current clipboard content and places it into the URL field. Clipboard data is not stored or sent anywhere.

Internet Access

The app uses internet access only to resolve shortened or redirected links so it can show the correct app for a URL. No personal data is sent during these requests.

Donations

The app includes an optional Donate feature powered by Google Play Billing. Donations are one-time, user-initiated, and handled entirely by Google Play. The app does not see, collect, or store any payment details. You can use the app without donating.

Third-Party Services

OpenInApp does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them.

Changes to This Policy

Any updates to this policy will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
